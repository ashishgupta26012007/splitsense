About SplitSense

An autonomous agent that reads your group expenses, settles them with the fewest possible payments, and keeps everyone accountable - with no manual math required.

01 How it works

SplitSense runs a four-step pipeline: it parses your plain-English expenses (including unequal, custom splits), calculates a debt-simplified settlement plan using a verified Python algorithm, checks its own math for correctness, and drafts friendly payment reminders - all without any manual calculation from you.

02 Built for groups

Create groups for trips, flats, or friend circles. Invite people via link or QR code, assign roles, and approve join requests. Every group keeps a live running balance - always up to date, even across dozens of expenses and partial settlements.

03 Stay organized

Categorize expenses automatically, search and filter your history, attach receipts, and track spending with weekly and monthly breakdowns - including a full year-long spending heatmap. Export any settlement as a CSV or a polished PDF statement.

04 Built with

Flask, SQLAlchemy, and an LLM-powered parsing engine - with every number cross-checked in Python, never trusted blindly. Designed to feel calm and precise, like a well-kept ledger.
